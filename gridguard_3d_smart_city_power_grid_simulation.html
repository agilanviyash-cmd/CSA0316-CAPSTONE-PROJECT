GRIDGUARD: Adaptive Power Grid Fault Localisation and Recovery

GridGuard demonstrates how classical graph theory, priority heaps, and traversal algorithms solve real-world smart-grid self-healing problems. When a substation experiences a fault, the system isolates the failed node, prioritizes life-critical infrastructure (Hospitals), and dynamically reroutes power from neighbouring substations without exceeding thermal capacity limits.

MODULE 01

3D City & Electrical Grid Network Creation

Constructs a 100-building urban smart grid partitioned into 10 zones using Three.js. Models residential homes, hospitals, and industrial factories aligned along city avenues, wired to 10 substations interconnected via an undirected Graph and Adjacency List.

MODULE 02

Manual Fault Detection & Localisation

Simulates circuit breaker trips across any combination of Grids 1–10. Immediately isolates the faulty node, marks all 10 dependent buildings in RED, recalculates city-wide KPIs, and inserts affected buildings into a Min-Heap Priority Queue.

MODULE 03

Neighbouring Grid Search & Adaptive Recovery

Executes Breadth-First Search (BFS) from the faulty substation to discover active neighbouring grids with spare capacity, supplemented by Depth-First Search (DFS) for multi-hop fallback routing. Allocates power fairly while strictly enforcing maximum kW limits.

Core Data Structures Implemented

1. Graph & Adjacency List

Stores 10 substations as vertices and high-voltage tie lines as edges in O(V + E) space for instant neighbor lookup.

2. Priority Queue (Binary Heap)

Orders affected buildings by criticality: Priority 1 (Hospitals) → Priority 2 (Industrial) → Priority 3 (Residential).

3. Breadth-First Search (BFS)

Finds the nearest active neighbouring substations hop-by-hop to minimize transmission losses during load transfer.

4. Depth-First Search (DFS)

Explores deeper interconnected grid chains when immediate 1-hop neighbors reach full capacity.

5. Queue (FIFO)

Manages BFS frontier exploration and sequences step-by-step animated switching operations.

6. Structured Arrays

Stores the 100 building entities and 10 substation records for O(1) indexed access and real-time 3D state syncing.

Educational Disclaimer: GridGuard is an academic computer science and data structures simulation designed for educational visualization. It models idealized power distribution rules and does not interface with or control real-world electrical SCADA hardware.
